# simedw 14 个实验跑完才读文献

一年前。

simedw 想着 MIDI + iPhone 实时补全。

不是「先读 literature 再 implement」。

是「自己想 + 自己跑」。

- 14 个实验
- 一年前
- 不读 literature
- 不 implement 他人 research
- 自己跑出来更快乐

---

## simedw 怎么说

> 「Almost a year ago, I started tinkering with an idea. Fourteen experiments later, it is finally at a point where I am happy enough with it to write about.」

> 「I deliberately avoided reading too deeply into them at first. I wanted the fun of working through the problem myself, rather than just implementing someone else's research. Only afterward did I go back and compare my approach with the existing literature.」

simedw 不想 implement 他人 research。

simedw 想自己跑出 fun。

---

## 对比

- Stanford 走 paper-first
- simedw 走 experiment-first
- Anticipatory Music Transformer 团队 = 论文先 + 蒸馏 + 引用
- simedw = 自己跑 + 14 个实验 + 完事才读 literature

Stanford 是先写论文再做实验。

simedw 是先做实验再读 literature。

顺序反过来。
