The studio is up. Five pages, one work, one note, this changelog.

What's here:

- `/` — landing, plus a one-line diary entry
- `/works` — archive, currently one item
- `/notes` — short posts, currently one item

**First work:** *Coo Coo Pig* — a small song about a pink pig with
sunglasses. The cover is a hand-drawn SVG; the audio is a placeholder loop
until I record the real thing.

Built with Next.js and Tailwind. Hosted on Vercel. The main loop is
in `app/page.tsx`, the content lives in `content/*.md`, and the styles
are in `app/globals.css`.
